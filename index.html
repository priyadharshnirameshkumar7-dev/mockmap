Open Theme Focus: Quantitative LULC & Environmental Fragility

Rapid urban sprawl, infrastructure expansion, and agrarian transformation across Nepal’s intermontane valleys present acute ecological trade-offs. This study quantifies land transition dynamics across nine standardized land-cover classes, utilizing cloud-free Sentinel-2 MSI surface reflectance paired with high-precision topographical metrics.

1. Sprawl Monitoring: Detecting urban expansion along transport arteries and riparian fringes.
2. Forest Protection: Safeguarding community forestry networks and upper watershed buffers.
3. Hazard Avoidance: Linking slope steepness (>30°) and river corridors to restrict vulnerable construction.

Geodetic and Projected Coordinate Reference System (CRS) Policy

Enforced in GIS Engine

Calculations of hectares, meters, euclidean buffers, and slope derivatives are never executed in unprojected EPSG:4326. Nepal spans across UTM zones 44 and 45 divided by the 84°E meridian:

West of 84°E Meridian

WGS 84 / UTM zone 44N

EPSG:32644
  • Central Meridian: 81°E
  • False Easting: 500,000 m | False Northing: 0 m
  • Coverage: Sudurpashchim, Karnali, Western Lumbini
  • Units: International Meters (Planimetric)
East of 84°E Meridian

WGS 84 / UTM zone 45N

EPSG:32645
  • Central Meridian: 87°E
  • False Easting: 500,000 m | False Northing: 0 m
  • Coverage: Bagmati, Gandaki, Koshi, Madhesh
  • Units: International Meters (Planimetric)
Mathematical Area Implementation: Projected Polygon Area is computed via the Gaussian Shoelace algorithm applied directly to UTM $(X_{\text{easting}}, Y_{\text{northing}})$ vertices: Area (ha) = 0.5 × |∑ (X_i · Y_{i+1} − X_{i+1} · Y_i)| ÷ 10,000

Proposed Output Allocation Categories

Very High Priority (80% - 100%): Optimal suitability, low slope, good access, zero hazard overlap.
High Priority (60% - 79%): Favorable conditions with minor gradient or infrastructure constraints.
Moderate Priority (40% - 59%): Requires site-specific engineering mitigations or drainage works.
Low Priority (20% - 39%): Marginal suitability, high excavation costs, or poor accessibility.
